Service route schedule

Fittings and fixtures

View and select fittings / fixtures

One count per placed record across all levels. Independent Fittings and Fixtures layers in Plan and 3D. Symbols mark location only. Add connection points in the selected item inspector. Linked route ends follow item/point movements; the legacy related-route field remains a reference.

Service connections

View connection checks and points

All levels/layers included. Green: linked geometry. Orange: unassigned or record review. Red: missing reference, gap, system mismatch or exceeded user-entered limit. 1 mm modelling tolerance; product suitability and system design require qualified review.

Service product envelopes

View product dimensions and clearance records

User-entered product dimensions and six clearance margins. Separate Plan/3D overlays; record supplier/designer evidence. Compare recorded zones in Service clearance checks. Other clashes and regulatory compliance require separate review.

Service route cross-sections

Review dimensions, sources and gross envelopes

Outside dimensions only. Gross straight segment envelopes are not pipe capacity, material quantities or orders; bend overlap is not deducted. Route totals and segment rows describe the same envelopes.

Physical route / body checks

Review physical overlaps and incomplete dimensions

Red: an affected straight segment has positive volume overlap with a recorded body. Amber: plan projection overlaps without positive 3D overlap. Highlighted envelopes are full affected segments, not clipped intersection solids. Z-range gaps are envelope ranges, not minimum clearances. Connected entries require review; no Australian system compliance is assessed.

Service route / body review

Review centreline crossings and incomplete bodies

Recorded route centrelines against dimensioned product bodies, across all levels. Red: centreline inside body. Amber: plan crossing without positive 3D entry. Connected items remain for review. Route thickness, clearances and Australian compliance are not assessed.

Service clearance checks

Review overlaps and missing clearance inputs

Recorded service clearance zones only, across all levels/layers. Red: volume overlap. Amber: plan intersection with no positive vertical overlap. Boundary contact alone is excluded. Other building objects, route thicknesses, product access and compliance require separate review.

BC DESIGN · DRAFT MATERIAL PLANNING

Stock-length allocation

Enter measured flat-ended pieces and candidate stock lengths. No model geometry is converted to fabrication cuts or changed.

Scope and Australian reference guidance

Up to 32 plans, 100 demand/stock rows per plan, 1000 pieces and 1000 candidate bars per plan. Lengths up to 1,000,000 mm with up to three decimals. Blank required dimensions keep the whole plan pending. Each plan remains independent of building takeoffs and other stock plans.

Business Queensland construction timber guidance and WoodSolutions flooring specification guidance were checked 27 October 2026 for product/specification review context. No numeric code requirement, supplier stock, suitability or compliance is inferred.

Member end-cut / mitre references

Independent full-face end-plane studies for recorded horizontal rectangular house floor members. No source geometry or takeoff deductions.

Modelling limits

256 studies; source length/width/depth up to1,000,000mm; coordinates/elevation within±1e9mm; centre setbacks0–1,000,000mm; plane slope angles±80°. Every longitudinal corner edge must remain at least0.001mm. Arithmetic boundary epsilon1e-6mm is not a site tolerance. Full-face end planes only: no notches, holes, profiles, kerf, machine setup, joint fit, capacity or purchase approval.

Plate and fastener references

Measured envelopes with explicit project dimensions. Empty inputs stay unassessed; saved members and manual fitting quantities are unchanged.

Scope and source

Plate bodies are rectangular; shanks are straight circular cylinders with flat ends (24 drawing facets, analytic volume). No holes, heads, threads, washers, bending, joint cuts, automatic attachment, embedment or capacity checks. Intersections and duplicates are not deducted. Limits:128 assemblies,64 shanks per assembly,1024 shanks per project. Sizes0.001–1,000,000mm; coordinates/elevations±1,000,000,000mm,local offsets±1,000,000mm,yaw±360°,tilt±90°.

Simpson Strong-Tie Australia connector fastener guidance checked27October2026 supports recording exact fastener/connector and installation evidence. No product, load rule or numerical fixing criterion is selected.
